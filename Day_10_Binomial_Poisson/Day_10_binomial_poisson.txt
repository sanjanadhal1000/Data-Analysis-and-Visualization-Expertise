-----------------------
Binomial Distribution:
-----------------------

Used when:
- you repeat an experiment n times.
- Each trial has only 2 outcomes - success or failure.
- Success probability = p

Example: Flipping a coin 10 times: how many heads?

-----------------------
Poisson Distribution:
-----------------------

Used when:
- you count the no. of events happening in a fixed interval.
- Events happen independently.
- Average rate = λ (lambda).

Example: Avg. 4 customers enter a shop per minute, how many customers will enter the shop today in 1000 minutes?
