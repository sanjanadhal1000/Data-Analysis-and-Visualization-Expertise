Q2. For the situations below, write H₀ and H₁

> A nutrition bar company claims their bar has 10g protein. You suspect it has less.

    H₀ (Null Hypothesis):
        H₀ : μ = 10           (the average protein content is 10g)

    H₁ (Alternative Hypothesis):
        H₁ : μ < 10           (You suspect it has less than 10g protein)

    This is a left-tailed test.

> A teacher believes Class A and Class B perform equally well in maths.

    H₀ : μA​ = μB (mean scores of class A and class B are equal)

    H₁ : μA​ != μB​ (mean scores of class A and class B are not equal)

    This is a two-tailed test.

> A coin is suspected to be biased towards heads.

    Biased towards heads -> probability of heads is more than 0.5

    H₀ (Null Hypothesis):
        H₀ : p = 0.5 (the coin is fair)

    H₁ (Alternative Hypothesis):
        H₁ : p > 0.5 (the coin gives heads more than expected - biased)

    This is a right-tailed test.
