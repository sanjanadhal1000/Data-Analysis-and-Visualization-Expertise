Q3. Decide which test to apply:

> Compare mean height of men vs women → ?

    Independent two-sample t-test 
        * Comparing means of two Independent groups. (men != women)

> Check whether average delivery time is 30 minutes → ?

    One-sample t-test
        * Comparing one sample mean against a known value (μ = 30)

> Is the proportion of customers who click an ad > 20% → ?

    One-sample Proportion z-Test
        * Checking a proportion 
        * Comparing sample proportion p to 0.20
        * Direction is p > 0.20 (right-tailed)
