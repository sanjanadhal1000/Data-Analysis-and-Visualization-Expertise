---------------------------------------------
Normal Distribution (Gaussian Distribution):
---------------------------------------------

- Symmetric and bell-shaped.
- Mean = center of the curve.
- Standard Deviation (σ)= width or spread
- Most data lies near the mean.

- 68-95-99.7 Rule:

For a normal distribution,

68% of data lies between mean ± 1σ.
95% lies between mean ± 2σ.
99.7% lies between mean ± 3σ.
